ELEMENT J — DOCUMENTATION OF EVALUATION

J1 — Involvement of Stakeholders and Field Experts
Documentation of project evaluation is by:

5 = multiple stakeholders and multiple demonstrably-qualified field experts
4 = two stakeholders and two demonstrably-qualified field experts
3 = multiple stakeholders or demonstrably-qualified field experts
2 = two stakeholders or demonstrably-qualified field experts
1 = one stakeholder or field expert (who may or may not be demonstrably qualified)
0 = no stakeholder or field expert

J2 — Comprehensive Evaluation
Documentation of project evaluation __________ aspects of your design or engineering design process.

5 = addresses two or more aspects
4 = addresses at least one aspect
3 = may not be complete in any aspect
2 = is incomplete or overly general in all aspects
1 = is insufficient in all aspects
0 = is nonexistent or minimal in all aspects

J3 — Synthesizing the Project External Evaluation
Documentation of project external evaluation is summarized __________.

5 = in a consistently-specific, detailed, and thorough way
4 = in a generally-specific, detailed, and thorough way
3 = in a somewhat-specific and detailed way, but may not be thorough
2 = in a somewhat-specific and/or detailed, but incomplete or overly general way
1 = in a sparse manner, with few specifics/details
0 = minimally or is missing
