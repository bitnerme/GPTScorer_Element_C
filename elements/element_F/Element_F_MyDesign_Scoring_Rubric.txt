Element F: Consideration of Design Viability

F1. Design viability judgment.
A judgment about whether the proposed design is realistic and viable.

Score 5:
The design is clearly realistic and well supported with credible evidence.

Score 4:
The design is generally realistic and adequately supported with credible evidence.

Score 3:
The design is only somewhat or sometimes realistic and is only partially supported with credible evidence.

Score 2:
The design may be somewhat unrealistic and/or is inadequately supported with credible evidence.

Score 1:
The design is unrealistic or not supported with credible evidence.

Score 0:
The design is unrealistic and is not supported with credible evidence.
