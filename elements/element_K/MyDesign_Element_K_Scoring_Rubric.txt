ELEMENT K — REFLECTION ON THE DESIGN PROJECT

K1 — Clear and Comprehensive Summary
The project designer provides __________ of the project.

5 = a robust and comprehensive summary of each step
4 = a clear and comprehensive summary of the major steps
3 = a generally clear and adequately-developed summary of most steps
2 = a sparse summary of some steps
1 = an overly-general summary
0 = no summary

K2 — Value Judgment
A value judgment is __________ provided for project steps.

5 = consistently and robustly
4 = consistently (for major steps)
3 = consistently (for major steps, with one or two addressed in only a cursory manner)
2 = partially (one or two addressed in only a cursory manner)
1 = partially and overly general or superficial
0 = not attempted

K3 — Inclusion of Lessons Learned
Lessons learned reflections are __________.

5 = substantive and are clearly useful to others attempting engineering design in the future
4 = summarized and are clearly useful to others attempting engineering design in the future
3 = summarized and at least most are useful to others attempting engineering design in the future
2 = included in part and some are useful to others attempting engineering design in the future
1 = minimally included and at least one is useful to others attempting engineering design in the future
0 = not attempted, unclear, and/or unlikely to be useful to others attempting engineering design in the future
