MyDesign Element G: Construction of a Testable Prototype

Overview:
Element G evaluates how well the student explains their prototype iteration, constructs a prototype capable of testing design requirements, enables testing or modeling of attributes, and justifies attributes that cannot be tested.

All subelements (G1–G4) are scored independently on a 0–5 scale.


G1. Final Prototype Iteration Explanation
The final prototype iteration is:

5 – Clearly and fully explained
4 – Clearly and adequately explained
3 – Adequately explained
2 – Somewhat explained but with gaps
1 – Only minimally explained
0 – Unclear or missing


G2. Construction of the Final Prototype
The final prototype iteration is constructed such that objective data on design requirements can be determined:

5 – For all or nearly all design requirements
4 – For many or nearly all design requirements
3 – For some design requirements
2 – For at least a few design requirements
1 – For at least one design requirement
0 – Not applicable to any design requirements and/or functional claims


G3. Testing or Modeling of Attributes
Attributes of the unique solution that can be tested or modeled are addressed:

5 – All attributes
4 – Most attributes
3 – Some attributes
2 – A few attributes
1 – No more than one attribute
0 – None


G4. Addressing Non-Testable Attributes
Justification is provided for attributes that cannot be tested or modeled and require expert review:

5 – Well-supported justification
4 – Generally supported justification
3 – Adequately supported justification
2 – Developing justification
1 – Insufficient justification
0 – No justification
